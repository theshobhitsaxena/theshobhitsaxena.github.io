Who I am

I’m Shobhit Saxena, a BCA graduate with a growing interest in the intersection of software, artificial intelligence, data and user-focused digital products.

My approach is practical: learn a concept, build something with it, understand what works, identify what can be improved, and keep iterating.

Alongside technology, I am pursuing LLB, which adds another dimension to my interest in systems, public services, information and the way technology can make complex processes easier to understand.

How I work & learn

I enjoy moving between different layers of a product—from interface and user experience to Python logic, APIs, data persistence and AI integration.

I also use modern AI-assisted development tools as a coding copilot for exploration, boilerplate, debugging and experimentation while keeping the product vision, feature direction and problem-solving process central to my own work.
